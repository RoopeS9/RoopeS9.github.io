# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 2 November 2026*

Hermes mail is a personal tool that Roope S. runs for himself. It is not offered to anyone
else. Only the Google accounts of its owner are connected to it.

## What it accesses

- With Google's `gmail.readonly` permission, it reads the owner's messages and their metadata
  (sender, subject, date, labels).
- It cannot send, delete or change email.

## How the data is used

- Messages are processed on the owner's own server to sort them: urgent mail, to-dos, newsletters.
- Before any text is sent to a language model, it is filtered. Addresses, phone numbers, links,
  account numbers and similar details are removed. Messages marked private are never sent at all.
- The filtered text goes to a language model on servers in the EU, under a zero-data-retention
  setting, which means the provider does not store it.
- The results (labels, short summaries, to-do items) stay on the owner's server.

## Sharing and retention

- No data is sold or shared with anyone, and none is used for advertising.
- The exact text sent to the language model is kept on the owner's server for 30 days.
- Labels, summaries and to-dos stay there until the owner deletes them.

## Limited use

Hermes mail's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
